Hey there! Welcome to Evergarden org, the home of the [Evergarden colorscheme][evergarden] and all the cool things that come with it. :seedling:

evergarden is all about keeping Evergarden alive and spreading its cozy vibes across different apps, editors, and tools. We’re here to make sure you get a beautiful, consistent experience everywhere. ✨

Evergarden is a colorscheme inspired by lush forests, enchanted groves, and the calm of nature.
It was also inspired by the lively art of The Legend of Zelda: The Minish Cap.
It’s designed to be easy on the eyes, elegant, and just plain nice to look at—whether you’re coding, writing, or just staring at your terminal for fun (we won’t judge).
